    Welcome to PianoPilot

    It plays songs on Roblox pianos for you. Three quick steps and you're playing.

    1. Sit at a piano in Roblox
      Any Roblox piano works; Visual Pianos is a good place to start. Close the chat box, or the notes get typed into it.
    2. Run the calibration test
      Six short tests check how your piano handles black keys, fast notes and the pedal, and fix the settings for you.
    3. Add your first song
      An MP3, a MIDI file or a text sheet. PianoPilot sets it up for you, then press Play and click into Roblox.

      How PianoPilot works

      PianoPilot presses the keys of a Roblox piano for you, in time with a song.

      Play a song

      1. Sit at a piano in Roblox
        Close the chat box first, or the notes get typed into it.
      2. Pick a song here and press Play
        Or press Space. Best for this song (under the title) picks good settings for you; Speed, Play like and the rest are below the song map.
      3. Click into the Roblox window
        It counts down from 3, then plays (change the countdown in Settings). Pressing F6 inside Roblox starts right away.
      4. Click out of Roblox to pause
        It never types into other windows. Click back into Roblox to carry on.

      Hotkeys

      These work while Roblox is in front. In PianoPilot itself the F keys work too, while Home, End, Page Up and Page Down keep their usual jobs (scrolling, moving through the song list). Change them in Settings.

      Adding songs

      • Any file: press Add a song (top left) and drop it in, or drop it anywhere on this window. MP3s and videos are turned into songs; piano-only recordings come out best. MIDI files are added as they are. Each new song gets set up for you (Settings, New songs).
      • Text sheets like [tu] y o | p: Add a song, then Paste a piano sheet. Or drop a .txt sheet in.
      • A whole folder of recordings: Add a song, then Import a whole folder.
      • Deleting a song: open it and press Delete (right side, under the title). It goes to the Recycle Bin, so you can get it back from there.

      Playlists and shuffle

      • Play, Shuffle and Smart shuffle sit above every list: all songs, favorites, a playlist, even search results. The first song starts and the rest go in the queue.
      • Smart shuffle plays your favorites and songs you haven't heard in a while more often, and songs you skip less often. On a playlist it mixes in a few similar songs, marked ✦.
      • Right-click any song to play it next, queue it, or add it to a playlist. Make playlists in the Playlists tab.
      • Keep playing (in the Queue tab) carries on with Smart shuffle when the queue runs out.

      If something sounds off

      • Start here: run the calibration test in Settings. It fixes most problems for you.
      • A song sounds messy: press Best for this song under its title. Undo puts it back.
      • Black keys sound like white keys: in Settings, open Advanced and raise Black keys to about 20 ms.
      • Fast notes blur together: in Settings, open Advanced and raise Key hold time.
      • Fast songs drop notes: Roblox reads keys once per frame, so keep it at a steady 60 fps (lower Roblox's graphics quality first). If your PC and screen can, raise Maximum Frame Rate in Roblox's settings to 120 or more: Roblox then reads keys twice as often.
      • The camera flips on black keys: turn off Shift Lock Switch in Roblox's settings.
      • Nothing gets pressed: if Roblox runs as administrator, right-click PianoPilot.exe and choose Run as administrator.
      • Some notes are missing: the song goes past the piano's 61 keys. Press Best for this song, or try Best fit and 88 keys under More controls.

      About

      The first time you open PianoPilot.exe, Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info, then Run anyway. Windows shows this for apps that aren't digitally signed by a known publisher. PianoPilot.exe only starts pianopilot.py with Python, and its source is in the launcher folder.
